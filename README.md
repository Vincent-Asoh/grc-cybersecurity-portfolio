## How to review

Download and extract the complete ZIP, then open its root README.md. Each project contains its own README, methodology, populated CSV/Markdown artifacts, source references, diagrams and interview notes. The repository publishes the deliverables as downloadable archives; the project files are inside those archives.

The portfolio demonstrates GRC, NIST RMF, NIST SP 800-53, NIST CSF 2.0, ISO/IEC 27001 mapping, vulnerability management, CVE/CVSS analysis, Nessus and Qualys VMDR workflow concepts, asset discovery, authenticated scanning, false-positive validation, patch/remediation management, risk acceptance, security controls, audit/compliance and executive communication.

Transferable risk skills include defining scope, assessing business impact, reconciling evidence, assigning owners, tracking deadlines and escalating unresolved issues. No employment history or real-world results are implied.

## Quality and scope

The fixed scenario snapshot is September 30, 2026. The complete archive includes 15 CSV files with 117 records, a cross-project traceability map, a quality report and a SHA-256 file manifest. Archive integrity, local links, risk arithmetic, finding/action joins and vulnerability metrics were checked before publication.

Framework and CVE references are real; operational data and decisions are simulated. Planned risk reductions remain separate from current risk. Screenshots are optional authorized-lab additions, not fabricated vendor evidence.
